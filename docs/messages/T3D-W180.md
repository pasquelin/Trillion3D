Level: **warning**. Group: [Maya ASCII](../COMPILER_ERRORS.md#maya-ascii).

An object-space normal map was dropped; glTF reads tangent-space normals.

**Cause.** A `bump2d` reading object-space normals (`bumpInterp` 2); glTF's `normalTexture` is tangent-space, and converting needs the pose at draw time

**What to do.** Bake a tangent-space normal map.
