Level: **error**. Group: [Global](../COMPILER_ERRORS.md#global).

A glTF primitive is not made of triangles.

**Cause.** A primitive `mode` other than triangles (points, lines, strips or fans the reader does not expand).

**What to do.** Triangulate the mesh in its authoring tool; points and lines are not drawn.
