# T3D-W129 — `usd-texture-scale-unsupported`

Level: **warning**. Group: [USD](../COMPILER_ERRORS.md#usd).

A texture scale or bias glTF cannot carry was dropped.

**Cause.** A `scale` or `bias` no glTF factor carries (glTF multiplies, never adds): a non-zero `bias`, a `scale` differing across colour channels, or an alpha `scale` other than one

**What to do.** Bake the scale and bias into the texture.
