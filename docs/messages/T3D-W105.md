Level: **warning**. Group: [Images](../COMPILER_ERRORS.md#images).

A glTF image's buffer view reads past its buffer, so its texture levels are not baked.

**Cause.** An image buffer view whose range leaves its buffer.

**What to do.** Export the model again; its buffer is truncated.
