# T3D-W066 — `exr-channels-unsupported`

Level: **warning**. Group: [Images](../COMPILER_ERRORS.md#images).

OpenEXR channels other than R, G, B and A were refused, so the texture falls back to white.

**Cause.** Channels other than `R`, `G`, `B` plus optional `A` — extra or other names, 32-bit integers, subsampling

**What to do.** Export the image with R, G, B and optional A channels only.
