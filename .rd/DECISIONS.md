# Decision log

Date / step: 2026-10-03 / shadows, translucent casters
Choice: keep the engine's coloured transmission layer on top of the virtual shadow maps.
Trade-off: virtual shadow maps alone cast no coloured shadow through glass (translucent materials do not cast into them unless masked), so the transmission layer is kept on purpose.
Reason: no image loss versus the engine before the port (boss's decision, option B).
Method: a coloured transmission texture seen from the light, multiplied per pane, storing the depth of the nearest pane; a receiver behind that depth multiplies its shadow factor by the stored colour.
Source: boss's decision; engine's former transmittance layer (packages/sdk-browser/src/gpu/shadow/transmittance.ts).
Result: done in the engine (vsm/transmission*.ts), any scene / pane count / colour / light type. Full-resolution transmission pages (128x128, the depth page's texels), one per VSM physical page and slice (dynamic/static) that a translucent caster covers, taken on demand from a slot pool through a per-page indirection (starts at 64 slots, 8.4 MB words + 8.4 MB atlas, doubles on overflow). Panes follow the same static/dynamic rule as every caster (mobility + settle; blended rows are no longer forced moving) and the opaque raster's cull/expand; a slice is cleared/redrawn exactly when the VSM redraws that slice; an opacity/colour change invalidates the pane's pages by its mobility. Colour multiplied by atomic compare-exchange, nearest depth by atomicMax; old blendTransmittance and caster selection reused. Read in vsmShadowFactor (blend/water/reflections) and, for the SMRT mask path, from the same page-table lookup in the resolve; light colour x shadowTransmission. shadows-through-see-through-panes 1728x1117 DPR2: middle pane shadow absent at 0, full at 1; at rest 0 VSM pages rendered, transmission 0.06 ms; an opacity change re-renders ~190 pages for ~4 frames at ~0.8 ms. drive-a-car / shadows-from-the-sun: no blended caster, nothing allocated, 0 ms. Plain (non-transmissive) panes cast untinted shadows (old logic: tint only for transmission > 0).

Date / step: 2026-10-03 / shadows, receiver on curved faceted surfaces
Choice: the VSM projection reads every map from the engine's shadow receiver: the pixel's point on the Phong surface of its vertex normals (`shadowReceiver().offset`, visibility/shader/shadingPoint.ts) and the normal bias + receiver-plane depth slope on the pixel's triangle plane turned to the shading normal's side (`shadowBiasNormal`).
Trade-off: projecting from the depth-buffer point and biasing with the geometric normal is the plain alternative; it is not used here because it gives the facet-serrated band described below.
Reason: no image loss versus the engine before the port; the old path read every map from this receiver. Without it a smooth-shaded low-poly sphere/cylinder shadows itself in a hard, facet-serrated band above its shading terminator (from-clay-to-chrome).
Method: the receiver text is the lighting's own (`receiverOffsetWgsl`, now with a bind-group argument), bound as group 2 of the projection when its 3 storage buffers fit beside the VSM tables' (else the depth-buffer point and the geometric normal); applied before the normal bias. Marking unchanged (offset ≪ a page).
Result: from-clay-to-chrome 1728x1117 DPR2 (lamps frozen): the hard mid-sphere band is gone, terminator matches the develop reference. a-field-of-pebbles, a-city-from-rules unchanged elsewhere.

Date / step: 2026-10-03 / shadows, receiver written once
Choice: the opaque resolve (shadeWgsl.ts), which already decodes the pixel's triangle, writes the shadow receiver into an 8 B/px rg32uint target (plane octahedral 2x10 bits; offset 3 signed 13-bit mantissas over a shared 5-bit exponent) (`receiverTargetWgsl.ts`, `SurfaceBuffer.receiver`); the VSM projection reads it instead of decoding the triangle from the visibility buffer per pixel. The resolve's lighting decodes only when translucent casters exist; demand and probe keep the decode.
Trade-off: none added (the receiver itself is the deliberate choice above). Reverses #1410's "no offset target" for the projection's sake: +8 B/px (≈62 MB at 3456x2234).
Reason: cost. The projection's own decode took ~1-1.5 ms (drive-a-car 2.2-2.4 → 1.5 ms; a-world-of-blocks 2.8-2.9 → 1.5-1.8 ms, interleaved A/B 1728x1117 DPR2).
Result: same receiver: an in-shader check (decode vs target, |Δoffset|>20 µm or plane dot<0.9999 → black) showed no mismatched pixel on from-clay-to-chrome (packing error ≤ 4e-4 of the largest offset component, ≤ 4e-3 rad on the plane); panes 0 px vs the decode within A/A.

Date / step: 2026-10-03 / projection reconstructs with the jittered view (audit M9)
Choice: the projection's view-to-clip is the frame's raster projection with its TAA jitter (`taaRenderMatrix` · view⁻¹), as the depth it reconstructs from was drawn; marking already reads the deferred view's jittered matrix.
Why: the reconstruction must use the same matrix as the depth it reads, otherwise the reconstructed position is off by the jitter.
Result: no change on a still (unjittered) image (A/B within A/A on shadows-through-see-through-panes).

Date / step: 2026-10-03 / projection skips tiles no light reaches (audit §4.4)
Choice: each 8x8 projection group ORs, per light, whether any of its pixels participates (in rect, lit surface, in the light's radius/cone, not back-facing: `vsmLightParticipates`), and the whole group skips a light none of its pixels is in, writing the out-of-light result. Background, off-screen and out-of-range tiles trace nothing.
Why: this is exact culling at tile granularity, so a pixel's own result is unchanged.
Result: crates-under-a-spotlight 1.2-1.4 → 0.8-0.95 ms (interleaved A/B, 1728x1117 DPR2); sun scenes unchanged within noise (the sun covers every surface tile).

Date / step: 2026-10-04 / shadows, sharp sun shadows (staircase)
Choice: the sun's clipmap runs the highest quality level's resolution LOD bias, -1.5 at rest and -1.5 moving, still clamped to >= 0 after the screen term; and the screen term never reads less than the display width, whatever scale the frame is drawn at.
Parts: A, the bias value -1.5 (value without derivation written here). B, the clipmap is no longer sized for the drawn view alone: its width is floored every frame at the display width, so dynamic resolution no longer coarsens the sun's shadows. Local lights keep the drawn size.
Reason: jagged, wavy sun shadow edges (frame-the-model, gears-and-pulleys), two stacked causes: the code-default bias 0 made our clipmap 2^0.774 = 1.7x coarser than the -1.5 bias at full width; and with renderScale 'auto' at 0.5 (the boss's panel: 75 requested / 95 cached pages, projection 2.03 ms, GPU 15.5 ms) the screen term read 1728 px instead of 3456: bias 1.774, one level more, texel 2x, steps 13 px apart.
Method: `VSM_SUN_LEVEL_BIAS` and `_MOVING` = -1.5 (`vsm/constants.ts`); `VsmViewport.minScreenWidth`, fed by `planVsmFrame` with `gpu.displaySize[0]`, enters only the clipmap's width max (`vsm/clipmap.ts`); local lights read `width` / `height` as before. A bias change invalidates no cache (the clipmap cache key is the direction and the first level). Pool stays 2048 pages.
Source: out/staircase/VERDICT.md (two diagnoses judged and re-run on 458f45a3ef, 3456x2234, against develop f3cb2f33c).
Result (the verdict's numbers, gears-and-pulleys default camera, edge RMS in device px, converged; T = texel period): crate vertical / crate bottom / weight vertical: before, scale 0.5 0.43 / 0.85 / 0.44 (T 13 px), scale 1 0.21 / 0.39 / 0.19 (T 7 px); -1.5 bias + display width at scale 0.5 0.09 / 0.13 / 0.09, the -1.5 bias at scale 1 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.09, no texel period left; the -1.5 bias alone at scale 0.5 0.23 / 0.46 / 0.23 (still scalloped: why part B); develop (atlas + PCF) 0.07 / 0.20 / 0.06 at 0.5 and 0.04 / 0.06 / 0.04 at 1. Exact at scale 1: 0.45 / 0.65 / 0.43 -> 0.31 / 0.39 / 0.33 (develop 0.05 / 0.09 / 0.05). 10-90 % edge width unchanged converged (2 / 3 px), 1 / 2 px exact (develop's): sharper, not blurred. frame-the-model default view (obelisk on grazing ground at 51 m), upper / lower: scale 0.5 0.55 / 0.49 -> 0.10 / 0.10; the -1.5 bias at scale 1 0.089 / 0.086 (develop 0.27 / 0.25 at 0.5, 0.15 / 0.14 at 1). Cost: pages at rest (gears) 76 -> 637 at scale 0.5, 204 -> 638 at scale 1, 1410 of 2048 free at worst; marking, page management and projection within noise; raster grows only where casters move or the cache is invalidated (pages rendered over a ~900-frame load: 7.7k -> 77k at 0.5, 24.6k -> 80.8k at 1). Bound: the pool-pressure global LOD bias (above 85 % pool load, up to +2); no other knob. The gap left to develop at scale 1 is aperiodic SMRT dither noise for the TAA lane, not resolution.
